CRIT
SIL-only validation framed as HIL — methodological mislabelling
Abstract, Section IV intro, Section V title, Section VI title
The abstract states "Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) validation against the SIL-generated 50 Hz grid". The test methodology is a Software-in-the-Loop framework running on real ESP32 silicon via serial commands — that is closer to processor-in-the-loop (PIL) or firmware-in-the-loop (FIL) than true HIL. True HIL requires real analogue signals injected into real hardware front-ends. Calling it HIL when the paper explicitly says the ADC acquisition front-end is bypassed will draw scrutiny from power-systems reviewers.
Fix: Use a consistent term like "Firmware-in-the-Loop (FIL)" or "Processor-in-the-Loop (PIL)" throughout, and add a sentence in Section IV clarifying the distinction from analogue HIL benches per IEC 60255-1 §7.
